Notes

Small text is the hold action; the corner legend is what Shift + that key types. Dashed keys pass through to Base. Home row mods: A ⇧, S ⌃, D ⌥, F ⌘ and J ⌘, K ⌥, L ⌃, ; ⇧, with Flow Tap 150 ms (a mod key pressed right after another key is always a tap). Hyper on Esc (Caps position), Enter, G and H. Fn has every symbol on the letter rows with mirrored brackets ([ ] on S L, { } on D K, ( ) on F J), HHKB arrows on [ ; ' /, media on F7–F12, ⏭ on Backspace and ⏯ on Enter. Nav has Backspace on Esc, arrows, Home/End and PgUp/PgDn on the left and a numpad on the right, with . on the right ⌘. Both Shifts stay Shift on Nav. Layer 3 is spare (no lighting on this board).
